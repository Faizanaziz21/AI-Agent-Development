# Master Services Agreement (Template)

## Liability
Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim, except for breaches of confidentiality.

## Service levels
Monthly availability below 99.9% entitles the customer to service credits of 5% of monthly fees per 0.1% shortfall, capped at 30%.

## Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice.
