# Refund and Credit Policy

## Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of an invoice for unused annual licences. Service credits for SLA breaches are calculated according to the Master Services Agreement.

## Approval thresholds
Refunds up to $1,000 may be processed by support. Refunds above $1,000 must be approved by a finance manager. Refunds above $25,000 are processed manually by the finance team.

## Process
1. Confirm the invoice number and the licences being returned.
2. Record the refund reason on the ticket.
3. Issue the refund through the billing system and send the customer confirmation.
