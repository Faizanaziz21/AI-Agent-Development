# Enterprise Device Control Platform — Product Overview

## What it does
The Enterprise Device Control Platform lets IT and security teams control which USB storage, phones (MTP), Bluetooth and other peripherals can connect to Windows and macOS endpoints. Policies are defined centrally and enforced by a lightweight agent, with a full audit trail of every device connection and file transfer.

## Ideal customer profile
Our best customers are organisations with 200 to 5,000 employees in regulated or data-sensitive sectors: financial services, healthcare, pharmaceuticals, legal services, public sector suppliers and energy. Strong buying signals include hybrid workforces, recent data-loss incidents, ISO 27001 or Cyber Essentials Plus certification programmes, large field or contractor workforces, and existing Microsoft Intune or Jamf device management.

## Pricing
List price is $6 per endpoint per month billed annually, with volume discounts above 2,500 endpoints.

## Integrations
Native integrations with Microsoft Intune, Jamf, Okta, CrowdStrike and major SIEM platforms via syslog and webhooks.
